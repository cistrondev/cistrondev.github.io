About the name

cis·tron

A cistron is the stretch of DNA that does one complete job: it carries the instructions for a single protein. The word comes from biology's cis–trans test, and cis is Latin for “on this side”. That's the idea behind every app here: each does one job well, and does it on your side, on your iPhone, not on someone else's server.
