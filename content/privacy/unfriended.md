# Privacy Policy for Unfriended

**Effective date: 31 October 2026**

Unfriended ("the app") is designed to work on your device from data that you
provide. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does
not do with your information.

## The short version

**Unfriended does not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share any of
your personal data. We have no servers and no account system.** There are no
user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party tracking. Your
Facebook data is analyzed and stored only on your iPhone. **The app sends
nothing of yours anywhere.** Its only connection is Apple's App Store, for
the optional tips (see "Tips" below).

## Your Facebook data

The app works from the official data export that **you** request and download
from Facebook yourself ("Download your information" in Accounts Centre). When
you import that file into the app:

- It is read and analyzed **entirely on your device**.
- The results (your friends, detected unfriends, name changes, removed
  friends, friend requests, followers, and related lists) are stored **only
  on your device** in the app's private storage.
- This information is **never uploaded to us or to any third party**. We have
  no server that could receive it, and the developer has no access to it.
- You control it completely: erase everything at any time with the **Reset All
  Data** button, or by deleting the app.

## Network access

Unfriended has **no online features**. It fetches nothing and sends nothing
of yours anywhere, so your Facebook data cannot leave your device through the
app. The one exception is Apple's App Store, which loads the prices of the
optional tips when you scroll to them and handles the payment if you leave
one. Nothing from your Facebook data is part of that.

## Information you enter

The app does not ask you to create an account or enter personal details such
as your name, email, or password. Any actions you take within the app, for
example dismissing an entry from a list, are stored **locally on your
device** and are not sent anywhere.

## Tips

Unfriended is **free**, with no subscription. It offers three optional tips, which are in-app purchases. A tip
unlocks nothing. To show their prices and to take a payment, the app uses
Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives
your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app keeps no record of your
tips. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking** of any
kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not employ any
third‑party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not follow you across
other apps or websites.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all of your data is stored only on your device, you are always in
control of it:

- Dismiss individual entries from within the app at any time.
- Tap **nuke everything** to permanently erase all imported data and detection
  history at once.
- Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data.

The developer holds no copy of your data and therefore cannot access, restore,
or delete it on your behalf.

## Children's privacy

Unfriended is not directed to children under 13, and the app does not
knowingly collect personal information from anyone of any age, as it collects
no personal data at all.
