# Privacy Policy for Sleep Sonar

**Last updated: 1 November 2026**

Sleep Sonar ("the app") records your night on your iPhone: it listens for snoring and sleep talk, and follows your
breathing with an inaudible sonar. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with
your information.

## The short version

**Sleep Sonar does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no servers, no
analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything the app hears is analysed on your iPhone, and everything it
keeps stays on your iPhone. The app has no network code and makes no network connections.

## The microphone

With your permission, the app uses the microphone **only while a night or a placement check is running**. iOS shows
the microphone indicator then. The microphone serves two purposes:

- it listens for snoring, sleep talk, coughs and other sounds, which Apple's built-in sound classifier labels on the
  phone;
- it hears the echo of the app's sonar: a sweep between 18 and 20 kHz, above what adults can hear, played from the
  phone's speaker. The echo from your chest shows your breathing and your movement. The sonar can be turned off.

The night is not stored as a recording. The app keeps a short clip of each sound it recognises, from three seconds
before the sound to two seconds after it (at most 45 seconds), so you can play it back in the morning. Everything
between those moments is analysed and discarded as it happens. Clips are never uploaded, transmitted, sold, or shared
with the developer or anyone else.

You can turn off microphone access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

## What the app saves

The app keeps, in its own storage on your iPhone:

- one record per night: for each minute, the breathing rate and distance the sonar measured, seconds of movement,
  the sound level, the volume and the strength of the sonar echo; the sounds it recognised, with their times; any
  breathing pauses; and a few lines describing the audio setup (sample rate, microphone, speaker);
- the clips described above, in a folder per night;
- your settings: whether the sonar is on, whether nights are saved to Apple Health, whether you have seen the
  introduction, and the dates on which the app asked you for a rating.

None of this is sent anywhere or accessible to the developer.

## Apple Health

If you turn on **Save to Apple Health** (it is off until you do), the app adds each night to the Health app on your
iPhone through Apple's HealthKit framework: the time in bed, the sleep estimate as time asleep and awake (Sleep
Analysis), and the breathing rate minute by minute (Respiratory Rate). A night can also be added on its own from
its report. These entries are then Apple Health's: you can see and delete them in the Health app, and deleting a
night in Sleep Sonar removes its entries too. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings › Health ›
Data Access & Devices › Sleep Sonar.

The app never reads anything from Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is
never shared with anyone.

## Ratings and email

Now and then the app may ask you to rate it, using Apple's standard rating prompt; Apple handles any rating you give.
**Rate Sleep Sonar** opens the App Store, and **Email the developer** opens a new message in your mail app with the
app and iOS version filled in. The app itself sends nothing: an email reaches the developer only if you send it.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking** of any kind. It does not use the Advertising
Identifier (IDFA), does not include any analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not follow you across other apps or
websites.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you are always in control of it:

- Touch and hold a night in the list to delete it together with its clips.
- Cancel within the first ten seconds of a night and nothing is kept.
- Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes everything it saved.

The developer holds no copy of your data and so cannot access, restore, or delete it for you.

## Not a medical device

Sleep Sonar is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. Breathing pauses are
shown for your information only. If you are worried about your breathing during sleep, talk to a doctor.

## Children's privacy

Sleep Sonar does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
