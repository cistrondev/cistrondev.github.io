# Privacy Policy for Jobsite Calc

**Last updated: 26 October 2026**

Jobsite Calc ("the app") is a feet-inch-fraction construction calculator with stair, rafter, roof, concrete,
materials and angle tools. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your
information.

## The short version

**Jobsite Calc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no servers,
no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app has no network code and makes no network connections.
Everything you type stays on your iPhone or iPad.

## What the app saves

The app keeps a few small things in its own storage on your device:

- your settings (fraction resolution, units, display formats, the stair and material defaults, appearance);
- a note of whether the one-time purchase has been made, so the tools open without asking the App Store each time;
- a count of how often the app has just been useful to you, which decides when it asks for a rating.

The calculator's tape, the list of your recent calculations, lives only while the app is open. None of this is
sent anywhere or accessible to the developer. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## The one-time purchase

The app is free to download. One in-app purchase, made through Apple, unlocks the tools for good. There is no
subscription and nothing renews. The purchase is handled entirely by the App Store: the app asks Apple whether
your Apple Account owns the unlock, and Apple answers yes or no. The developer receives no personal data from the
purchase. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
The unlock can be shared with your family through Family Sharing, which Apple manages.

## Sharing from the app

When you share the tape, the app hands the text to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The app does
not see or keep where you sent it.

## Ratings and reviews

Occasionally the app asks iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt. Whether and how that prompt appears is
decided by iOS, and anything you type into it goes to Apple, not to the app.

## Links

The Settings screen has links to this website and to an email address for support. Opening them leaves the app
and uses Safari or your mail app. Email you send reaches the developer and is used only to answer you.

## Children's privacy

The app has no accounts and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
