# Privacy Policy for Timegrapher

**Effective date: 6 July 2026**

Timegrapher ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device. This
policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your
information.

## The short version

**Timegrapher does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**
There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no
third‑party tracking. The app's only network activity is the optional market
price check described below, which you trigger yourself. Everything you enter
or measure stays on your iPhone.

## Microphone

Timegrapher uses the microphone to listen to a mechanical watch and analyze the
sound of its ticking in real time. The audio is processed live on your device
and is **never recorded, saved, or transmitted**. Nothing from the microphone
leaves your iPhone, and no audio is retained after a measurement ends.

## Photos and camera

If you choose to add a photo to a watch, the app accesses your camera or photo
library only to obtain the image you select. That image is stored **only on
your device**, as part of the app's local data. Photos are never uploaded or
shared by the app.

## Information you enter

Any details you add — such as brand, model, reference number, serial number,
year, notes, and your saved measurements — are stored **locally on your
device** using Apple's on‑device storage (SwiftData). This information is not
sent anywhere and is not accessible to the developer.

## Market prices (Chrono24)

If you use the optional market‑price check, the app loads the public Chrono24
search results for your watch's reference in a built‑in browser view — exactly
as if you had opened the page yourself — and computes the median asking price
on your device. The request goes directly to Chrono24 and contains only the
search text (the watch's brand and reference number). The developer receives
nothing, stores nothing, and cannot see what you looked up. Chrono24's own
privacy policy governs their website. If you never tap the fetch button, the
app makes no network connections at all.

## Purchases

Timegrapher offers an optional one‑time in‑app purchase ("Timegrapher Pro") that
unlocks unlimited watches. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the
App Store. The app does not receive, store, or process your payment details.
Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking** of any kind.
It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not employ any
third‑party SDKs, and does not follow you across other apps or websites.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored only on your device, you are always in control of it:

- Delete an individual watch or measurement from within the app at any time.
- Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data.

The developer holds no copy of your data and therefore cannot access, restore,
or delete it on your behalf.

## Children's privacy

Timegrapher does not collect any personal information from anyone, including
children.
