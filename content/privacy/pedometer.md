# Privacy Policy for Pedometer

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**

Pedometer ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy
explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your
information.

## The short version

**Pedometer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**
There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no third‑party advertising,
and no tracking. The app makes no network connections. Your health and motion
information, and everything the app works out from it, stays on your iPhone.

## Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

With your permission, Pedometer reads these categories from Apple Health:
steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed, active energy, and
walking speed.

This data is read **only to show today's totals, the charts, and the widgets
inside the app.** Specifically:

- Everything happens **on your device**. Your health data is never uploaded,
  transmitted, sold, or shared with the developer or any third party.
- Pedometer **does not** use your health data for advertising or marketing,
  and **does not** share it with third parties for advertising or data mining.
- Access is **read‑only**. The app never writes to, changes, or deletes
  anything in Apple Health.
- You choose which categories the app may read in Apple's Health permission
  screen, and can change that at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security ›
  Health › Pedometer.

## Motion & Fitness (Core Motion)

With your permission, Pedometer reads today's step count, distance, and floors
climbed from your iPhone's motion sensors. It does this to keep today's count
in the app and its widgets current, including when Apple Health cannot be
read. Like health data, this stays on your iPhone.

## Information kept on your device

The app saves its latest totals for today and your daily goals in storage
shared only between the app and its own widgets on your iPhone. They are not
sent anywhere and are not accessible to the developer.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no third‑party advertising, and no tracking**
of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not
include any third‑party SDKs, and does not follow you across other apps or
websites.

The app shows one link to LifeTrends, another app by the same developer.
Tapping it opens the App Store. The app sends nothing about you with it.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you are always in control of it:

- Revoke Health or Motion & Fitness access at any time in the iOS Settings
  app. The app immediately stops reading that data.
- Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes everything it saved.

The developer holds no copy of your data and so cannot access, restore, or
delete it for you.

## Not a medical device

Pedometer is intended for personal interest only. It is **not a medical
device**, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Children's privacy

Pedometer does not collect any personal information from anyone, including
children.
