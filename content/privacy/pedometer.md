# Privacy Policy for Pedometer

**Last updated: 24 October 2026**

Pedometer ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy
explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your
information.

## The short version

**Pedometer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**
There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no third‑party advertising,
and no tracking. Your health, motion and location information, and everything
the app works out from it, stays on your iPhone and, where you allow it, in
Apple Health. The app's only network connection is the map, which Apple Maps
draws (see "Maps" below).

## Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

With your permission, Pedometer reads these categories from Apple Health:
steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed, active energy, and
walking speed, to show today's totals, the charts, the records and the
widgets; and, for the walks and runs you record, your heart rate (to show it
live and in the summary) and your weight (to estimate calories).

When you record a walk or run, Pedometer saves it to Apple Health as a
workout, with its route, its distance and its active energy. It writes
nothing else to Apple Health, and never changes or deletes anything there.
Deleting a recording in Pedometer leaves the workout in Apple Health.

Heart rate during a walk or run comes from a heart rate sensor you wear, such
as AirPods Pro 3 or a Bluetooth heart rate monitor, through Apple Health.

Specifically:

- Everything happens **on your device**. Your health data is never uploaded,
  transmitted, sold, or shared with the developer or any third party.
- Pedometer **does not** use your health data for advertising or marketing,
  and **does not** share it with third parties for advertising or data mining.
- You choose which categories the app may read and write in Apple's Health
  permission screen, and can change that at any time in Settings › Privacy &
  Security › Health › Pedometer.

## Location

With your permission, Pedometer uses your precise location **only while you
record a walk or run**, including while your iPhone is locked (iOS shows the
location indicator then). It uses it to draw your route and to measure
distance, speed and elevation. The route is kept on your iPhone, in the app
and, with your permission, in Apple Health. It is never sent to the developer
or anyone else. The app does not use your location at any other time.

## Maps

The map of a walk or run is drawn by Apple Maps. To draw it, your iPhone loads
map images for the area shown from Apple, as it does in any app that shows an
Apple map. Apple's privacy policy covers that. Pedometer sends nothing else
with it, and the developer receives nothing.

## Motion & Fitness (Core Motion)

With your permission, Pedometer reads step count, distance, floors climbed
and changes in altitude from your iPhone's motion sensors. It does this to
keep today's count in the app and its widgets current, including when Apple
Health cannot be read, and to count the steps and the climb of a walk or run.
Like health data, this stays on your iPhone.

## Information kept on your device

The app saves its latest totals for today and your daily goals in storage
shared only between the app and its own widgets on your iPhone. It saves the
walks and runs you record (their route, times and numbers) in its own storage
on your iPhone. None of this is sent anywhere or accessible to the developer.

## Sharing a walk or run

When you choose to share a walk or run, Pedometer makes a picture with a map
of the route and its numbers, and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes
only where you send it. Pedometer shares nothing on its own.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no third‑party advertising, and no tracking**
of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not
include any third‑party SDKs, and does not follow you across other apps or
websites.

The app shows one link to LifeTrends, another app by the same developer.
Tapping it opens the App Store. The app sends nothing about you with it.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you are always in control of it:

- Revoke Health, Location or Motion & Fitness access at any time in the iOS
  Settings app. The app immediately stops using that data.
- Delete a walk or run in the app to remove it from Pedometer. Workouts saved
  to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.
- Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes everything it saved.

The developer holds no copy of your data and so cannot access, restore, or
delete it for you.

## Not a medical device

Pedometer is intended for personal interest only. It is **not a medical
device**, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Children's privacy

Pedometer does not collect any personal information from anyone, including
children.
