Questions and answers

How can I see who unfollowed me on Instagram?

Instagram doesn't show who unfollowed you. Your Instagram data export lists everyone who follows you on the day you download it. Unfollowers compares two of these exports and shows who left.

  1. Download your data export from Instagram, with Followers and following in JSON format. Here is how.
  2. Import the ZIP file into Unfollowers. You see right away who doesn't follow you back.
  3. A few weeks later, download a new export and import it too. Unfollowers lists who unfollowed you since the first one, with the date each change was found.

Each new export you import adds to your history.

Is there a safe unfollow tracker without login?

Unfollowers works without a login. It never asks for your Instagram password and never signs in to your account. It reads the data export that you download from Instagram yourself.

Many follower trackers ask for your Instagram password and then use your account in the background. Instagram warns that letting unauthorized apps into your account puts it at risk. With Unfollowers, your password stays with you, and the app never takes any action on your account.

Your export file is read and stored on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

How do I download my Instagram data export?

In the Instagram app, open your profile and tap the menu. Then go to Accounts Center › Your information and permissions › Export your information › Create export. On some accounts, Accounts Center is called Meta Account. Choose Export to device. Pick Followers and following, the whole date range and the JSON format, then tap Start export. Instagram tells you when the file is ready, usually within minutes.

Can I see who blocked me on Instagram?

Instagram doesn't report blocks, so no app can know for sure. When someone blocks you, they disappear from your followers and from the accounts you follow at the same time. Unfollowers flags these accounts as probably blocked. This only works for people who followed you and whom you followed.

An account that was deleted, deactivated or renamed disappears in the same way. The profile check (the vibe check) asks instagram.com whether each username still exists, and moves accounts that are gone to a separate list.

Is Unfollowers free?

Unfollowers is free to download. The free version shows who doesn't follow you back, who you don't follow back, and your mutuals. A one-time purchase unlocks everything else, including the lists of who unfollowed you and who probably blocked you. There is no subscription. The app runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Does anything leave my iPhone?

Your export file stays on your iPhone. The app reads and stores it there, and the developer runs no servers. The one feature that uses the internet is the profile check. It asks instagram.com whether an account still exists and sends only that username, without signing in. It runs when you tap the vibe check, and, once the app is unlocked, for up to 30 new accounts after each import. Privacy policy.
