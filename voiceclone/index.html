Voice Clone

Text to speech in your own voice.

Coming soon · iPhone 15 Pro or later · iOS 18 or later

Coming soon to the App Store

Record a short sample of your voice, type any text, and hear it read in your voice. The voice model runs on your iPhone, so your recordings and clips stay with you. There is no account and nothing to buy.

  • Your voice in 15 seconds

    Talk for 10 to 15 seconds in your normal voice. The app learns your voice from that one sample, on your iPhone.

  • Type anything

    Type what it should say and tap Generate. A long text starts playing as soon as its first sentence is ready.

  • 22 languages

    Record once, in any language, then make clips in 22 languages. When your text looks like another language, the app offers to switch.

  • Works offline

    The first time, the app downloads the voice model, about 1.9 GB. After that it works in airplane mode.

  • Your clips

    Every clip is saved with its text and language. Play it, share it, save it to Files, or delete it.

  • No account, no credits

    There is nothing to sign up for and no credits to buy.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Read the privacy policy

  • Your voice sample, your text, and your clips stay on your iPhone.
  • The voice model runs on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Each clip is labelled as AI-generated in its file information.
  • No account, no analytics, no tracking.

Needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later, for its 8 GB of memory. Clone only your own voice, or the voice of someone who agreed to it.
