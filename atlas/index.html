Atlas

Every country you've been to, from your photos.

Coming soon · iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Coming soon to the App Store

On iPhone Duo

Unfold it for a globe of your travels.

Atlas finds the countries you've been to in the photos on your iPhone and puts them on a world map. It reads only the location and date saved with each photo, on the phone. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded.

  • Your map, filled in

    Every country in your photos, on a world map you can zoom into. Tap any country to see its name and how many times you've been.

  • Every trip

    Each visit to a country, with its dates. Tap a trip to see its photos, and hide any that aren't from it.

  • Lived here

    Countries with 90 or more days of photos within a year are marked as places you've lived, in a deeper colour.

  • Stats

    Trips and days away, countries per year, your busiest years and where you went, the countries you go back to most, and your longest trips.

  • Share your map

    A picture of your map and flags for an Instagram post or Story, or all your flags as emoji for your bio.

  • Fewer false visits

    Pictures saved from Instagram, WhatsApp and other apps are left out. You can add, edit or remove any country yourself.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your iPhone.

Read the privacy policy

  • Atlas reads the location and date of your photos on the phone.
  • There is no account, and nothing is uploaded.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.
