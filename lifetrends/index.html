Questions and answers

Is there an app that finds patterns in my Apple Health data?

LifeTrends reads the Apple Health history already on your iPhone and tells you, in plain sentences, what is in it: that you walk more on weekends, sleep less on Sunday nights, or climb more stairs in June. Tap any finding for the gap, the sample sizes and whether it still holds this year. See it on the App Store.

How does LifeTrends decide what counts as a pattern?

A difference only becomes a finding when it is large enough to matter and unlikely to be chance. One unusual week stays an unusual week. Correlations between two measurements appear only when they pass a significance test. The Info tab explains how every conclusion is reached.

What data does it use?

Steps, distance, stairs and location trends work with just an iPhone. Heart rate, sleep, exercise and daylight come from a wearable such as Apple Watch. Blood pressure needs a connected monitor, and weight or calories need entries in Apple Health. LifeTrends uses whatever you have and skips the rest.

Can it tell how travel affects my sleep and activity?

Yes. With optional photo access, LifeTrends reads the date and location saved with your photos, never the pictures, and matches them to cities built into the app. It can then show, for example, that you walk more in New York or sleep shorter and earlier after a long flight east.

Is LifeTrends free?

The Summary tab is free and covers your last two years. LifeTrends Pro is a one-time purchase, never a subscription, and adds the Statistics tab, with every measurement charted and correlation plots, and analysis of your full history.

Does LifeTrends upload my health data?

No. Health data is read, analysed and kept on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no analytics. The only network request happens if you tap Current Location while outside the built-in city list: then one approximate position goes to Apple to name the city. Privacy policy.

Is it a medical app?

No. LifeTrends describes patterns in measurements you have already recorded. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
